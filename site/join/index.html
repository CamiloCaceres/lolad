What is Lolad?
Free and open source · Mac and Windows

A friend invited you to Lolad.

Lolad runs a small team of AI assistants on your own computer. Install it, paste your invite link, and you’re connected: you can message each other, and the assistants you each choose.

    Already have Lolad? Skip to step 3.

    1. 1

      Download Lolad

      Lolad runs on a Mac or a Windows computer. Copy your link (step 3), open it on your computer, and download it there.

      Download for Mac Download for Windows

      If Edge says the file “isn’t commonly downloaded”, click the … next to it, choose Keep, then Keep anyway. New apps get this message until more people have downloaded them.

    2. 2

      Install it and open it

      Open the file you downloaded and follow the short setup. It walks you through your first assistant.

      This early version isn’t signed yet, so the first time you open it your computer asks you to confirm. The install guide shows each step.

      1. Double-click Lolad-Setup.exe in your Downloads.
      2. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” window appears, click More info, then Run anyway. It appears because this early version isn’t signed yet.
      3. Click Install. Leave Open Lolad now ticked and click Finish.

      Lolad opens and walks you through setup. You don’t need an administrator password.

      Stuck? The Windows install guide shows each step.

      1. Open Lolad.dmg in your Downloads and drag Lolad onto Applications.
      2. Open Lolad from Applications. The first time, your Mac says it can’t verify Lolad, because Apple hasn’t reviewed this early version yet. Click Done.
      3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Lolad, then confirm.

      Lolad opens and walks you through setup. Your Mac remembers this, so you only do it once.

      Stuck? The Mac install guide shows each step.

    3. 3

      Paste your invite link into Lolad

      At the end of setup Lolad asks “Got an invite from a friend?” Paste the link there. Or later, open Sharing and use Got an invite?

    Your invite stays in this browser tab. This page doesn’t send it anywhere. It works once, so don’t share it with anyone else.
