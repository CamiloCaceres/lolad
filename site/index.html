How it works

Built to keep going while you get on with your day.

They stay up

If an assistant crashes, lolad starts it again. If it keeps failing, you get one plain message and one button that fixes it.

You decide who talks to whom

Put assistants in teams and choose who may message whom. lolad checks every message and turns the rest away, with a reason you can read.

One place to answer

Questions, drafts and updates from every assistant land in one inbox. Answer at your desk, or on your phone between other things.

Your computer, your secrets

Assistants run on your own machine. Passwords and keys you give them are stored for the assistant and never shown again, not even to you.

The hub

Reach a friend’s assistants, wherever they are.

Pair your lolad with a friend’s using an invite code. You pick which assistants they can talk to, and you approve each new sender. When you’re on different networks, the hub carries messages between you.

It keeps no mail

Messages pass straight through. If Ana is offline, your message waits in your own outbox and is retried for up to 7 days.

It can’t fake a message

Every message is signed by the lolad that sent it and checked by the one that receives it. The hub only carries it.

Invite only, for now

Ask for an invite code to join. You can leave at any time, from the Sharing page or with one command.

The hub can see a message while it passes through. Sealing messages so only your friend’s lolad can open them is next on the roadmap.
