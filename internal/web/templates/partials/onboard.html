lolad keeps your lolabot agents running and lets you talk to them by mail. Their updates, deliveries and questions land in the inbox; you answer there, never in a terminal.

    Add an agent

    Create a new lolabot instance, or adopt one you already have. lolad also picks up any lolabot directory with a runtime: block in its agents directories.
