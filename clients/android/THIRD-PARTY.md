# Reuse and dependencies

Native Material theme resources, conversation layout patterns, Gradle wrapper and signing-script structure were adapted from Milo’s read-only `maestro-lite/apps/android` client. Its GPL-3.0 license is retained in LICENSE. No Termux, AI Maestro protocol, notifications, portraits, keystores or tokens were copied.

Resolved through Gradle: AndroidX, Material Components, OkHttp, Markwon and ZXing Android Embedded (Apache-2.0); JUnit 4 (EPL-1.0, test only), JSON-java (public domain, test only). Gradle wrapper is Apache-2.0. No CDN assets or runtime analytics.
